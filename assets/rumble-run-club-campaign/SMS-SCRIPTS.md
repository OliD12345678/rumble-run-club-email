# Rumble Run Club — SMS Companion Scripts (ClubReady Connect)

SMS runs alongside the emails, it does not replace them. Same-day reminders convert far better as text. Keep sends to RSVPs and members only, not the cold 12k list.

**Rules of thumb**
- Under 160 characters = 1 segment = 1 credit. Every script below is counted.
- Marketing texts need opt-out language. Reminders to people who RSVP'd are transactional and generally don't, but Connect appends your compliance footer automatically. Leave it on.
- Never send before 8 AM or after 8 PM.
- Send from the studio line so replies land where Lydia can see them.

---

## 1. RSVP confirmation (automated, fires on form submit)

> You're in for Rumble Run Club! Sat 8:30am, Rumble Alpharetta. Arrive 8:15. Bring water. Protein shakes after w/ Project LeanNation. See ya then!

`137 characters`

**Setup:** this is the one worth automating. If the FormSubmit relay can hit a Connect webhook or Zapier, fire it instantly on submit. Speed-to-lead applies to events too, a confirmation inside 60 seconds massively lifts show rate.

---

## 2. Aug 6, Wed 5:00 PM — soft nudge (RSVPs + members)

> Rumble Run Club is this Saturday, 8:30am. Coach Lindsay's leading. All paces welcome. Reply YES and we'll save you a spot.

`121 characters`

**Why reply YES:** an inbound reply converts a passive RSVP into a commitment and gives Lydia a live conversation thread. Any reply also boosts your deliverability with carriers.

---

## 3. Aug 7, Fri 6:00 PM — 24-hour (RSVPs only)

> Tomorrow! Rumble Run Club 8:30am. Roll in at 8:15. Just bring water. Coach Lindsay's got the rest. See you in the morning.

`121 characters`

---

## 4. Aug 8, Sat 7:00 AM — morning-of (RSVPs only)

> Good morning! Run Club is at 8:30. Shoes on, water in hand, we're at the studio. Doors open 8:15. Let's go.

`106 characters`

**Timing:** 7 AM gives 90 minutes. Earlier feels aggressive, later is too late for someone still deciding.

---

## 5. Aug 8, Sat 12:00 PM — same-day thank you (attendees)

> That was a good one. Thanks for running with us today. Round two is Aug 15, 8:30am, Coach JP leading. Bring somebody with you.

`124 characters`

**Why same-day:** you are texting someone who feels great and is still in the glow. Highest-conversion moment of the entire campaign for booking the second one.

## 9. Aug 17, Mon 11:00 AM — conversion (attendees only)

> You showed up 2 Saturdays in a row. That's the hard part. Want to keep it going? [OFFER] Book here: [LINK] Reply STOP to opt out.

`~128 characters depending on your offer`

**Fill in:** keep the offer to 3-4 words in a text ("First class free" / "Intro rate this week"). Long offers belong in the email, the text just drives the click.

---

## 10. Aug 18, Tue 11:00 AM — comeback (RSVP'd but no-showed)

> Missed you at Run Club! No worries, life happens. Your first class is still on us whenever you're ready: [LINK] Reply STOP to opt out.

`~132 characters`

**Note:** this is the segment nobody texts and it is full of intent. They raised their hand, then didn't show. A no-guilt text 3 days later converts surprisingly well.

## Send volume estimate

| Send | Audience | Est. credits |
|---|---|---|
| RSVP confirmation | per submit | 1 each |
| Wed nudge x2 | RSVPs + members | ~2 x list |
| 24-hr x2 | RSVPs only | ~2 x RSVPs |
| Morning-of x2 | RSVPs only | ~2 x RSVPs |
| Same-day thanks | attendees | ~1 x attendees |
| Conversion + comeback | segments | ~1 x each |

If 300 people RSVP, budget roughly 1,500-2,000 SMS credits for the full campaign. Check your Connect rate before scheduling all of it.

---

## The one that matters most

If you only run one text from this list, run **#5, the same-day thank you.** It hits attendees while the endorphins are still up, and it books the second Saturday. Everything else is reinforcement.
